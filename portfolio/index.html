Falling Short: Child Stunting in Rwanda

Rwanda nearly halved child stunting, from 51% in 2005 to 27% in 2025. At the current pace, it will reach about 22% by 2029, still short of the 15% NST2 target. This infographic shows who is still being left behind: toddlers aged 6–23 months, children in the North and West, and children from the poorest homes. It is built on Rwanda DHS 2025 data.
