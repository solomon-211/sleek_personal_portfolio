Aspire Leaders Program — Finally Get It Done!!!

Today, I am super thrilled to share that I have completed the Aspire Leaders Program — a Harvard-affiliated initiative designed to equip emerging leaders with leadership and social entrepreneurship skills to tackle real-world problems.

Solomon Thiong Garang Leek May 2025 5 min read Leadership
Aspire Leaders Program Graduate · Harvard-Affiliated · 2025

Today, I am super thrilled to share that I have completed the Aspire Leaders Program from Aspire Institute — a program designed to educate low-income, first-generation college students and underserved students with leadership and social entrepreneurship skills, among others, to become emerging leaders who are incredibly innovative and creative in tackling real-world problems.

What Is the Aspire Leaders Program?

The Aspire Leaders Program is a globally competitive leadership initiative designed for students who are driven to create meaningful change. Having attended and completed this program, I discovered and gained invaluable insights from masterclasses, peer-to-peer sessions, and general group discussions based on different topics shared by the moderators — sessions where participants share personal experiences, perspectives, and interests based on challenging real-world scenarios.

This program is not just about learning leadership theory — it is about developing the clarity, credibility, and critical thinking needed to lead with purpose in a rapidly changing world.

The Two Modules

This program has two distinct modules, each building on the other:

Module 1 — Personal & Professional Development

Module 1 focuses on personal and professional development through a strength-based learning approach. Drawing from leadership assessment and career-resources building co-created with Harvard Business School, it equips participants with credibility, clarity, and critical thinking in leadership. The goal is not just to learn frameworks — it is to understand your own strengths and how to apply them in the context of real leadership challenges.

Module 2 — Aspire Horizon Courses

Module 2 focuses on a dynamic shift in leadership, trust, AI, and community impacts through Aspire Horizon courses — designed, delivered, and offered by Harvard Faculty. This module challenged my understanding of how leadership intersects with emerging technologies and the pressing global challenges of today.

Key Insights & Takeaways

This program has been an incredible opportunity to learn and grow as an individual driven by effective leadership, community engagement, impact, and innovation to solve real-world problems. A few key moments stand out:

  • AI & Business Transformation. Professor Tugba Efendigil facilitated masterclasses on how AI is redefining businesses and operations in this digital era. Her emphasis was exceptional — AI is not only improving things, but revolutionizing and transforming how businesses and organizations operate, innovate, and compete to address pressing global challenges.
  • Peer-to-Peer Learning. The peer-to-peer sessions played a crucial role in improving my ability to give and receive constructive feedback, engage in open dialogue, and ask meaningful questions. Learning from peers across different countries and backgrounds was one of the most valuable parts of the experience.
  • Community of Changemakers. The program brought together brilliant, like-minded individuals who envision a prosperous future — people learning, exchanging ideas, and discussing shared experiences in their passionate fields. That community is itself a resource I will carry forward.
"The Aspire Leaders Program is not just a transformative experience — it is a place to learn and grow as an individual driven by effective leadership, community impact, and innovation to solve real-world problems." — Solomon Thiong Garang Leek

Why This Matters to Me

Completing this program reinforced something I already believed: that leadership is not a title — it is a commitment to showing up, thinking critically, and taking action even when the path is unclear. The skills, perspectives, and connections I gained here will directly shape how I approach my work at ALU, my community initiatives, and my long-term vision for building technology that serves people.

Additionally, this program is an educational journey where changemakers who envision a prosperous future can interact, exchange words, ideas, and discuss shared experiences and interests in their passionate fields. The global network of leaders I connected with is a reminder that the problems we are each trying to solve are bigger than any one of us — and that collaboration is not optional, it is essential.

What's Next

I am continuing to build. The lessons from this program feed directly into everything I am working on — from software engineering projects at ALU to community development initiatives. The program confirmed that leadership and technology are not separate pursuits. They are the same work, done well.

To anyone considering applying to the Aspire Leaders Program: do it. Come with an open mind, a willingness to be challenged, and a genuine desire to contribute. The program will meet you there.
