About Me

Building meaningful digital solutions through thoughtful design, strong engineering, and purposeful leadership

Kigali, Rwanda

I am a Software Engineering student at African Leadership University (ALU) in Kigali, Rwanda, originally from South Sudan. My work sits at the intersection of software engineering, visual communication, and social impact.
