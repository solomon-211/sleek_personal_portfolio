How I Got a Full Scholarship to ALU — My Application Journey

Solomon Thiong Garang Leek Jan 2026 · 2 min read

I come from South Sudan, where most students don’t have access to higher-quality education. I chose African Leadership University because of the drive to cultivate African leaders to solve African problems, and I wanted to be educated to build in my home.

  1. First application

    I applied to ALU twice. My first application got me a partial grant. Although that was promising, it showed me that though I had been seen, I hadn’t been heard. My essays explained where I wanted to go, but not who I was. I didn’t give up, though; I persevered and had the courage to write my story once more.

  2. Second application

    This time around, in my second application, I was precise about a concrete real-world issue impacting my community- students being robbed of their learning due to fragile digital infrastructure. I was explicit about my strength as a student leader and upfront about my previous attempt. I shared that I was enrolled to study at a South Sudanese primary school during the day, and to prepare for university applications at night-that iteration strengthened my resilience. I carried this line in my pocket:

    “One minute on a book does not lie.”

    Noble Arem Riak

  3. That application was what won me the full-ride Mastercard Foundation Scholarship.

My guiding principles

  • Consistency

    I take my recovery seriously, and I am committed to the work every day.

  • Perseverance

    I continue until success and take the opportunity to do better next time.

  • Resilience

    I learn from every failure and become stronger.

  • Courage

    I embrace new opportunities and welcome feedback. I try again if it matters.

Today I am studying Software Engineering at ALU in Kigali, and these values influence the way I live. I allow my work to speak, and I also give back by paying it forward with other folks along the way. I have mentored over 500 applicants in East Africa, and over 10 of these students were awarded full-ride Mastercard Foundation Scholarships.
