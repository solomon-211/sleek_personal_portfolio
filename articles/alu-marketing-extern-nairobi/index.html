ALU Marketing & Outreach Team Extern — Nairobi Nexus

I am thrilled to have kicked off the Nairobi Nexus — a Signature Immersive Experience themed Learning by Doing: Through Education and Enterprises in East Africa.

Solomon Thiong Garang Leek May 2025 · 4 min read

I kicked off the Nairobi Nexus — a Signature Immersive Experience (SIE) themed Learning by Doing: Through Education and Enterprises in East Africa. This off-campus learning journey marks a defining moment in stepping outside the classroom and into the real ecosystem of innovation, enterprise, and community impact.

What Is the Nairobi Nexus SIE?

The Nairobi Nexus is a transformative, off-campus learning experience at ALU — designed to give students direct exposure to the world of innovation, technology, and entrepreneurship through immersion in East Africa's vibrant entrepreneurial ecosystem. The focus is on experiential learning: observing and learning from leading businesses and enterprises that started from grassroots initiatives and emerged as firms driving meaningful change.

Being assigned to extern with the ALU Outreach Team in Kenya is an incredible opportunity that profoundly resonates with me — a chance to get exposed to a real ecosystem of education and do what is expected, embrace what is possible, and contribute meaningfully.

Why This Experience Matters

Kicking off this off-campus journey marks the beginning of coming out of my comfort zone — gaining a better understanding of experiential learning and a glimpse into the world of innovation, technology, and entrepreneurship through immersing myself in the vibrant local impacts of Nairobi's ecosystem.

The Nairobi Nexus is not about sitting in lectures. It is about fieldwork, direct engagement, and getting your hands into the work. It is about enhancing hands-on skills by getting involved in real activities while gaining valuable experience and insights from excellent enterprises and businesses that are shaping East Africa's future.

The Extern Role — ALU Marketing & Outreach

As part of this experience, I am externing with the ALU Outreach Team — a team grounded in Kenya and committed to expanding the university's reach and impact across East Africa. This role directly aligns with my skills in visual communication, social media, and community engagement.

  • Producing visual content that communicates ALU's mission and impact across digital platforms.
  • Supporting campaign development for outreach initiatives targeting prospective students and community partners.
  • Learning from the team about strategic communication, brand positioning, and how to connect authentically with diverse audiences.
  • Expanding my network across the East African entrepreneurial and educational ecosystem.
"Embracing this opportunity is to learn, grow, and contribute to this notable team in Kenya." — Solomon Thiong Garang Leek

Looking Ahead

I am looking forward to expanding beyond my hands-on experiences, skills, and networking opportunities. This experience is not just about adding a line to a CV — it is about developing the kind of real-world perspective that makes you a better engineer, designer, communicator, and leader.

The Nairobi Nexus is a reminder that some of the most important learning happens outside the classroom. And I am here for it — fully present, ready to absorb, contribute, and grow.
