What I Saw at Light Academy Nairobi — College Transition & Why It Matters

Solomon Thiong Garang Leek Aug 2026 · 6 min read

Yesterday, I had the honor of visiting Light Academy — an international and national institution in Nairobi — as part of the Nairobi Nexus Program that I am currently attending with the ALU Recruitment Team.

The Alumni Wall

I was stunned to see the alumni wall and their results beside the school background — something that is quite rare to find in some institutions, especially secondary schools. What really amazed me was the great initiative and support this institution has offered, and continues to offer, to its current students: college preparation and transition.

This absolutely moved me — because I was so privileged to be one of the panelists navigating this institution's alumni through their college preparation and transition to world-top-notch and prestigious universities. We talked about what African Leadership University is literally all about, how to navigate the application process, and how to secure a full-ride scholarship at ALU under the Mastercard Foundation Scholarship.

What This Made Me Feel

I was moved — not because there are no institutions and programs that do similar initiatives — but because most student-led programs that are committed and dedicated to leveraging similar efforts significantly struggle to unleash the best out of high school graduates due to a lack of assistance and funding. They have the heart, the commitment, and the community trust, but not always the resources to match what students need.

On the other hand, some institutions that offer similar programs often provide little of what is truly expected of them. The gap between intention and execution is wide — and students pay the price.

What if other institutions also did the same, immensely supporting students to find the best colleges and universities? Not only graduating them and leaving them to struggle alone, but joining hands with communities, governments, and student-led programs committed to bringing out the best in students seeking higher education?

— A question I kept asking myself during the visit

The Bigger Picture

If that question becomes reality, I genuinely believe that most high school students and graduates who find themselves struggling with the college transition and preparation can be less affected by this major problem that appears minimal on the surface — but is absolutely outrageous in its impact.

College transition and preparation seem insignificant at first glance. But it is one of those things that stands as an orchestration of students' success and smooth transition into colleges and universities. The difference between a student who gets support and one who doesn't can define the next ten years of their life.

Gratitude & Commitment

I am incredibly proud of and grateful for the institutions and student-led programs and organizations that are committed to preparing and supporting students for a smooth transition to colleges and universities. What Light Academy is doing matters. What ALU's recruitment and outreach work is doing across East Africa matters.

And I am going to keep showing up — as a panelist, as a student, as someone who came from South Sudan and found a path through exactly the kind of support that many students still lack. If being in that room at Light Academy helps even one student take a step toward their future, it was worth every moment.
