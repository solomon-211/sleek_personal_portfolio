About me

Most doors open on the second try

I applied to African Leadership University twice. My first application earned a grant. My second earned the full Mastercard Foundation Scholarship. My goals had not changed. What changed was my approach: I stopped describing what I hoped to do and showed what I had already done.

That lesson still guides my work. I am a Software Engineering student at ALU in Kigali, working where technology, design and storytelling meet. Graphic design taught me to make ideas clear, and teaching primary school in South Sudan taught me patience and how to explain things simply.

My work speaks for itself. I designed the database behind InzoziHub’s e-commerce platform, built the brand and website for Sleek Nexus Creative, and contributed to HealthHub Bridge, a clinic management system for low-resource settings. Most recently, I co-created “Falling Short,” a data story on child stunting in Rwanda, told as a poster, a short video and an interactive website.

I also believe in opening doors for others. The right support can shape the next ten years of a student’s life. That is why I have guided more than 500 students across East Africa through the ALU application, and more than 10 of them have secured full Mastercard Foundation Scholarships.
