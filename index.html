About Me

Not just a developer. A problem-solver, a designer, and a leader — driven by one belief: technology should create real change for real people. That's the standard every project is held to.

Kigali, Rwanda

I am a Software Engineering student at African Leadership University (ALU) in Kigali, Rwanda, originally from South Sudan. My work sits at the intersection of software engineering, visual communication, and social impact.

I began with graphic design, which sharpened my eye for clarity, structure, and storytelling. That creative foundation naturally evolved into frontend development and Python, where I learned to transform ideas into scalable, usable solutions. Today, every project I take on follows one principle: deliver work that performs, communicates clearly, and creates measurable value.

Open to internships, collaborative projects, and opportunities where I can contribute, learn, and grow.
